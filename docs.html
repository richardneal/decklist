Getting Started

Welcome to decklist.app! This free tool helps you generate professional tournament registration sheets for Magic: The Gathering events in seconds.

Quick Start Guide

  1. Enter your information: Fill in your first and last name
  2. Add event details: Event name, date, and location
  3. Add your deck: Paste an MTGGoldfish, MTGTop8, Moxfield, Archidekt or Scryfall deck link and click "Import deck", or copy your decklist from MTG Arena, MTGO, Moxfield, MTGGoldfish, or TappedOut and paste it directly
  4. Download: Click "Download PDF" to get your tournament-ready registration sheet

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the MTG decklist generator?

Simply paste your deck from MTG Arena, MTGO, Moxfield, MTGGoldfish, TappedOut, or any supported format into the "Main Deck" field. The generator will automatically parse your cards, validate them, and create a tournament-ready WotC registration sheet as a PDF.

What deck formats are supported?

Our decklist generator supports MTG Arena, MTGO (Magic Online), Moxfield, MTGGoldfish, TappedOut, MTG Salvation, Deckbox "Print" format, and MWS (Magic Workstation) formats. You can paste decklists directly without needing to separate the sideboard.

How do I save and share my decklist?

Use the "Copy Permalink" button to copy a link that pre-fills all your deck information; on a phone it opens the share sheet instead. The link is built in your browser and carries the whole deck itself, so nothing is stored on a server or sent to a link shortener. You can share this link with others or use it to quickly load your deck later. You can also save decklists locally using the "Save Decklist" button.

Does the generator validate card legality?

Yes. Choose your format (Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper, Premodern, Old School, Commander, Duel Commander, Pauper Commander, PreDH or Oathbreaker) and the generator flags any card that is banned, not legal or over its copy limit, including the Vintage and Old School restricted lists and the cards Duel Commander bans as a commander. It also checks deck size: 60 cards and a 15-card sideboard for constructed, exactly 100 for Commander and its variants, exactly 60 for Oathbreaker counting the Oathbreaker and its signature spell, at least 40 for Limited. List the commander and any companion, or the Oathbreaker and its signature spell, in the sideboard. A Pauper Commander deck's commander is not checked, since the card data does not record which creatures were printed at uncommon. Left on Auto-detect, it guesses the format from the deck itself: a 100-card deck is Commander, a 40-card deck is Limited, and any other gets the narrowest constructed format it is legal in.

Is this decklist generator free to use?

Yes, decklist.app is completely free to use. Generate unlimited tournament registration sheets for Magic: The Gathering events without any cost or registration required.

Do I need to create an account?

No! decklist.app works entirely in your browser without any registration. Pasted decks are processed in your browser. Importing a link sends the deck source and ID to our import API; copying a permalink sends nothing anywhere, but the link itself carries the name, event and deck you entered, so share it only with people who should see them.

What does decklist.app count?

To find out when something breaks, the page counts a few outcomes and sends each one to decklist.app itself, with no cookies, IDs or outside services:

  • Downloads: the format, whether the sheet still had a warning or error, the kinds of deck problems (such as "toosmall" or "banned") and whether any cards weren't recognized (none, 1–2, or 3 or more)
  • Imports and uploads that fail: the deck site and the kind of failure (timeout, network or failed), the site of a link the importer can't read when it's one of a fixed list of deck sites ("other" otherwise), and an uploaded file's extension
  • Errors: a script error in the site's own code (its file, line and message, with any quoted text removed), a site file that failed to load, or a preview that couldn't be drawn

Each count also records the browser family (Chrome, Safari, Firefox…) and whether it came from a phone. Nothing you type is sent: not your name, event, deck name, cards or links. No IP address is stored. Importing a link also counts the import's outcome at our import API, without the deck's ID. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, whose Web Analytics also counts page loads, without cookies.

Supported Deck Formats

MTGO (Magic Online)

Export your deck from Magic Online and paste it directly. The format looks like:

4 Lightning Bolt
4 Counterspell
20 Island

Sideboard
3 Negate
2 Dispel

Lines marked SB:, as Magic Workstation writes them, go to the sideboard wherever they appear, and lines starting with //, such as its comments, are ignored.

MTG Arena

Export your deck from MTG Arena, or copy it in Arena's format from a deck site, and paste the whole export into the "Main Deck" field. Its headers sort the cards: Deck is the main deck, Sideboard the sideboard, and a Commander or Companion goes to the sideboard, commander first. The About block that names the deck is skipped, and so are the set code and collector number after each card:

About
Name Mono-Red Burn

Companion
1 Jegantha, the Wellspring (IKO) 222

Deck
4 Lightning Bolt (2X2) 117
4 Monastery Swiftspear (BRO) 144
20 Mountain (ANA) 1

Sideboard
1 Jegantha, the Wellspring (IKO) 222
2 Blood Moon (DMR) 123

Arena lists a companion in the sideboard as well, and it is counted once. Headers are read in any case, with or without a colon, so MAINBOARD: and Sideboard (15) work too. A Maybeboard section is left out.

MTGGoldfish

Paste the deck's address (mtggoldfish.com/deck/…) into "Import a deck", and its name fills in Deck Name. Or copy your deck from MTGGoldfish using the "Export" → "Text" option. Both main deck and sideboard will be parsed automatically.

Moxfield

Paste the deck's address (moxfield.com/decks/…) into "Import a deck". Commanders and a companion, or an Oathbreaker and its signature spell, are added to the sideboard, since Commander decks have none and a commander listed there stays easy to find; the maybeboard is ignored, and so is the sideboard of a deck with a commander, where Moxfield players keep their cuts. The deck's format is selected for you when it is one the generator checks, and its name fills in Deck Name.

You can also paste a deck copied from Moxfield's Export dialog, in plain text or for MTG Arena. Set codes, collector numbers and foil markers such as *F* are ignored, and the Commander, Companion and Sideboard headers are read as they are in an Arena export.

Archidekt

Paste the deck's address (archidekt.com/decks/…) into "Import a deck". The deck must be public. Cards in the Sideboard category go to the sideboard, as do the commander and a companion; and the Maybeboard and any other category left out of the deck are ignored, and so is the Sideboard of a deck with a commander, where Archidekt players keep their cuts. Each card is placed by its first category, as Archidekt itself counts it. The deck's format is selected for you when it is one the generator checks, and its name fills in Deck Name.

Scryfall

Paste the deck's address (scryfall.com/@you/decks/…) into "Import a deck". The deck must be public. A constructed deck keeps its mainboard and sideboard; a Commander deck's commander and its cards from outside the game, such as a companion, go to the sideboard, as do an Oathbreaker deck's Oathbreaker and signature spell. The maybeboard is ignored. The deck's format is selected for you when it is one the generator checks, and its name fills in Deck Name.

TappedOut

Use TappedOut's "Text" export format. Click on your deck, then select the "Text" view and copy everything.

Deck files

Have your deck saved as a file? Open the "Text file" tab in "Import a deck" and choose it, or drop it on the main deck box. A text list (.txt) from MTG Arena or any deck site, an MTGO .dek and a Cockatrice .cod all work. The file is read in your browser and never uploaded anywhere. "Undo import" puts back the deck it replaced.

Manual Entry

You can also manually type your deck in this format:

Quantity CardName
4 Lightning Bolt
3 Snapcaster Mage

Direct Linking

You can create custom URLs that pre-fill the decklist generator with specific information. This is perfect for:

  • Tournament organizers pre-filling event information
  • Deck building websites integrating with decklist.app
  • Sharing pre-configured decklists with friends

Import a Deck from Its Link

Put decklist.app/ in front of a deck's address on MTGGoldfish, MTGTop8, Moxfield, Archidekt or Scryfall, and the generator opens with that deck imported:

https://decklist.app/https://www.mtggoldfish.com/deck/6837920
decklist.app/moxfield.com/decks/…
decklist.app/https://www.mtgtop8.com/event?e=…&d=…&f=MO

The deck's https:// can stay or go, and nothing needs encoding. To combine an import with event or player details, use the deckimport parameter below instead.

Available URL Parameters

Simply add these parameters to the URL to pre-fill form fields:

Personal Information

  • firstname - Participant's first name (e.g., April)
  • lastname - Participant's last name (e.g., King)

Event Information

  • event - Event name (e.g., RCQ Minneapolis)
  • eventdate - Event date (e.g., 2027-12-31)
  • eventlocation - Physical location (e.g., Minneapolis Convention Center)

Deck Information

  • deckname - Deck name/archetype (e.g., "RUG Twin")
  • deckdesigner - Designer's name
  • deckformat - Format to check the deck against: standard, pioneer, modern, legacy, vintage, pauper, premodern, oldschool, commander, duel (Duel Commander), paupercommander, predh, oathbreaker, constructed (no ban list) or limited (defaults to auto-detect)
  • deckimport - URL-encoded MTGGoldfish, MTGTop8, Moxfield, Archidekt or Scryfall deck URL; imports automatically on page load. Explicit deckmain or deckside parameters take precedence.
  • deckmain - Main deck (use %0A for newlines, %20 or + for spaces)
  • deckside - Sideboard (use %0A for newlines, %20 or + for spaces)

Display Options

  • decksort - Sort order: alphabetical, manavalue, color, numeric, original, or type (defaults to type; a permalink includes it when it isn't the default)
  • disableediting - Prevent users from editing pre-filled fields

Examples

Tournament Organizer - Unregistered Player

https://decklist.app/?eventdate=2027-12-31&event=RCQ%20Minneapolis&eventlocation=Minneapolis%20Convention%20Center

Tournament Organizer - Pre-registered Player

https://decklist.app/?firstname=April&lastname=King&eventdate=2027-12-31&event=RCQ%20Minneapolis&eventlocation=Minneapolis%20Convention%20Center

Deck Building Website Integration

Pre-fill with a complete decklist (example shortened for readability):

https://decklist.app/?deckmain=4%20Lightning%20Bolt%0A4%20Brainstorm%0A20%20Island&deckside=3%20Negate%0A2%20Dispel

Complete Example

Everything together - personal info, event details, and full decklist:

https://decklist.app/?firstname=April&lastname=King&eventdate=2027-12-31&event=RCQ%20Minneapolis&eventlocation=Minneapolis%20Convention%20Center&deckname=Miracles&deckdesigner=CubeApril&deckmain=4%20Brainstorm%0A4%20Force%20of%20Will%0A4%20Island

Important: When creating links, replace spaces with %20 (or +) and use %0A for line breaks in deck lists.
